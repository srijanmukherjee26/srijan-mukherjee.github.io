01 · Research

Explore My Research

Permafrost & Active Layer Dynamics

Permafrost zonation, active-layer fluctuation and environmental controls across the Western Himalaya.

Remote Sensing & InSAR

Satellite observations, SAR backscatter and deformation time series for landscape and ground-subsidence studies.

Hydrogeology & Groundwater

Groundwater systems, recharge processes, connectivity, hydrogeological variability and vulnerability.

Hydrogeochemistry & Stable Isotopes

Hydrogeochemical signatures and δ¹⁸O–δD tracers to constrain water sources and mixing.

GIS & Geospatial Analysis

Spatial modelling, terrain analysis, environmental indices and geospatial integration.

Climate & Environmental Change

Climate–landscape interactions, ecosystem change and environmental monitoring in mountain systems.
